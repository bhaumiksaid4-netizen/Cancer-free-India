Introduction

Cancer is one of the leading causes of death in India. The vision of a Cancer-Free India focuses on prevention, early detection, and affordable treatment for all citizens.

Objectives

  • Understand cancer burden in India
  • Study national initiatives
  • Promote awareness and early detection
  • Suggest improvements

Causes and Risk Factors

  • Tobacco use
  • Alcohol consumption
  • Unhealthy diet
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Pollution
  • Genetic factors
  • Viral infections

Common Types of Cancer

  • Breast Cancer
  • Lung Cancer
  • Oral Cancer
  • Cervical Cancer
  • Stomach Cancer

National Initiatives (2026)

  • NPCDCS: Early detection and treatmentweb site of NPCDCS
  • Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY): Free treatment coveragePM JAY
  • National Cancer Grid: Quality cancer care
  • Tobacco Control Programs: Awareness & bans
  • Vaccination: HPV & Hepatitis B

Awareness and Early Detection

  • Regular check-ups
  • Screening programs
  • Self-examination
  • Public awareness campaigns

Challenges

  • Lack of awareness
  • Late diagnosis
  • High cost of treatment
  • Limited healthcare facilities
  • Social stigma

Solutions

  • Increase awareness campaigns
  • Improve rural healthcare
  • Promote healthy lifestyle
  • Encourage screening

Conclusion

With strong government initiatives and public awareness, India can reduce cancer cases and move toward a healthier future.
